Response style:

Executive but understandable.

Good example:

Flood Risk Assessment — Pune

Overall Risk: High

Key Findings:

* Several low-lying zones show flood susceptibility.
* Urban runoff is elevated due to dense construction.
* Critical infrastructure is exposed in some sectors.

Recommendations:

* Improve drainage.
* Install monitoring sensors.
* Prioritize emergency planning.

Bad example:

Telemetry friction and hydrological asymmetry indicate non-linear runoff vectorization.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Explain technical concepts clearly.
